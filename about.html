Our Story

Building the financial marketplace India can actually trust

RK Jha Group was founded on a simple frustration: financial products in India are sold, rarely explained. We set out to change that — one transparent comparison at a time.

Our Mission

Finance made transparent

We connect individuals, professionals and business owners with India's most reliable banks, NBFCs and insurers — and we do it by showing you every eligible option, not just the one that pays us the most.

Our advisory team combines deep product knowledge with a genuinely simple digital process, so decisions that used to take weeks of branch visits now take days, sometimes hours.

Regulatory Note

Compliance-first, by design

RK Jha Group operates as a financial products marketplace and advisory service. We are not a bank, NBFC or insurance underwriter. All loan products are extended solely by our RBI-regulated banking/NBFC partners, and all insurance products are underwritten by IRDAI-registered insurers, subject to their respective credit and underwriting policies. Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation.
